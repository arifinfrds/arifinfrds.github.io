Quran Firdaus

A modern Quran reading app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac — the diacritized Uthmani text and English & Indonesian translations read fully offline, with online audio recitation from 150+ reciters, a curated Dua collection, and a personalized For You tab that recommends verses based on the time of day. Architecture

A layered Model → ApiService → RemoteDataSource → Repository → ViewModel data layer feeds unidirectional-data-flow Compose screens via StateFlow. Playback runs on a Media3 foreground service with a shared, app-lifetime ExoPlayer instance so the mini-player survives tab navigation. Adaptive layouts use ListDetailPaneScaffold to collapse to single-pane push navigation on phones and expand to a permanent two-pane split on tablets and foldables from the same composables — no separate tablet codebase.

Since 2023

Milestones, Year by Year

2023

Quran Firdaus is born. First commit in January; the original SwiftUI master-detail reading experience takes shape, culminating in v1.4.0 that April and steady iteration through the year.

2024

A quiet year. No shipped releases — the calm before a much larger rebuild.

2025

The comeback, and the growth year. Development resumes in April with v1.4.x–v1.7.x and bookmarks. By autumn: Siri & App Intents, an on-device NLP-based sentiment filter for motivational verses, and a full audio player with reciter selection. The year closes with v3.0.0 on December 31 — an architecture refactor setting up everything that follows.

2026

The biggest leap yet — and an agentic one. v4.0.0 (July) brings diacritized Uthmani text, up to 4 Arabic reading fonts, and restores native macOS support. v4.2.0 (August) adds Home & Lock Screen widgets, scheduled notifications, and the personalized "For You" tab. Just as significantly, 2026 is the year the engineering workflow itself changes: feature planning and day-to-day development move to agentic AI coding with Claude Code skills.
